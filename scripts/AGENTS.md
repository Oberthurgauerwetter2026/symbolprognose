- Nowcast advection runs on the radar field sampled with a NOWCAST_PAD margin around the display bbox (soft-faded outer edge) and is cropped only afterwards, because cropping before advection drags a hard dry edge into the map.
- Forecast PNG phases use a source-sampled 840×400 terrain DEM; intensity is Catmull-Rom upsampled once to 840×400 before roughening and quantization (double interpolation of quantized steps caused plaid artifacts), because terrain detail must not change motion analysis or invent finer rainfall structure.
